## Repair Tracking Platform

**Project Overview:**
At the heart of the FixitAalst Repair Tracking Platform lies a robust system for managing electronic device repairs from intake to delivery. Built with NestJS and React (TypeScript) and backed by Supabase (Postgres), the application centralizes repair orders, inventory, customer records and invoicing to streamline workshop operations and reporting.

**Website Structure:**
The web app is organized into clear pages for operational flow:

Login / Register
Dashboard
Repairs (Create / Active)
Repairs — History
Customers
Devices
Parts / Inventory
Orders (Supplier)
Invoices
Settings / Users
Key Features:

Authentication & Roles: Secure JWT login with role-based access control.
Repair Workflow: Multi-step repair creation, status tracking (pending → in‑progress → completed → delivered).
Customer & Device Management: Link customers to devices and view full repair history.
Parts & Inventory: Stock levels, parts usage tracking, low-stock alerts and parts-to-device compatibility.
Supplier Orders: Create and manage supplier orders and incoming stock.
Invoicing & Payments: Generate invoices tied to repair orders and track payment status.
Dashboard & Analytics: Revenue, active orders, parts consumption, and repair status insights.
Search & Filters: Date ranges, status filters and pagination for large datasets.
Responsive UI: Tailwind-based interface with charts and modals for quick operations.
